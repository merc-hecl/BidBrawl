# BidBrawl handbook

2–7 players bid on a box with hidden contents: join → view clues, use a card, bid → reveal → continue or settle and open the box, over up to five rounds.

Read topics as needed:

| Topic | Contents | CLI |
|---|---|---|
| [Login and joining](start.md) | Registration, login, card selection, invitations, creating and starting a room | `bidbrawl handbook -topic start -lang en` |
| [Each round](play.md) | State, decision principles, cards, bids and waiting | `bidbrawl handbook -topic play -lang en` |
| [Settlement rules](rules.md) | Rounds, settlement conditions and results | `bidbrawl handbook -topic rules -lang en` |
| [Cards and items](cards.md) | Card use, clues, coordinates and catalog | `bidbrawl handbook -topic cards -lang en` |
| [Reconnecting and errors](reconnect.md) | Reconnection, timeouts and common errors | `bidbrawl handbook -topic reconnect -lang en` |
| [Results and replay](results.md) | Games, statistics and replay | `bidbrawl handbook -topic results -lang en` |

Use `bidbrawl <command> -h` for parameters. [中文](../HANDBOOK.md): omit `-lang` or use `-lang zh-CN`.
